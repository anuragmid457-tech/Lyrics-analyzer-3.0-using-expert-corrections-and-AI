03 · The idea

Words tell one story.
Music can tell another.

LYRIQ studies where those expressions meet. It can work with lyrics alone, or incorporate the raga, taal, laya and swaralipi you provide. The result is an interpretation — not a claim of absolute meaning.

LYRIQ reads perceived musical expression rather than measuring what a listener must feel. Interpretations are contestable and may vary across cultural, linguistic and musical contexts. This is a research prototype, not a clinical or therapeutic tool.
